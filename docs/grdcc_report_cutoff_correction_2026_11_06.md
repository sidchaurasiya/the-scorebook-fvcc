# GRDCC historical career cutoff correction

## Policy

For compatible additive career metrics, use the official cumulative ALL GRADES
Annual Report baseline through 2024/25 plus eligible public PlayCricket
contributions from 2025/26 onward. Frank Gray Shield is an included subset, not
an additional total. Do not replace missing source counters with zero or derive
whole-career rates from incomplete historical denominators.

The catalog contains 99 rows: 98 active exact-canonical metric baselines and one
quarantined identity mapping. The original 96 rows' values and source references
are preserved; the unsupported Phil-to-Percy row is marked identity_unresolved,
and three supported historical Runs baselines are appended. Original historical source rows, old extraction receipts, source seeds,
HS/BBI records and raw prepared snapshots are unchanged. Governance is applied
after prepared loading and is included in the career-serving cache signature.
