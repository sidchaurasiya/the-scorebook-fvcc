Desktop fit notes

Stacked grade cards keep Summer seasons readable. Each card is dense enough to scan several rounds without the page becoming a wall of giant match cards.

Use one compact card for one-grade seasons. Use stacked compact grade cards for whole-club multi-grade seasons. Keep scorecard context available but not visually dominant.
