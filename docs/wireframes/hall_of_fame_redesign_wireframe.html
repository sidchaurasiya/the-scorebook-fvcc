Design Recommendations

The decisions this redesign makes compared with the current Hall of Fame page.

Keep

  • All existing record calculations.
  • Premierships, leaders, records, fastest innings, seasons and detailed tables.
  • Player, season and scorecard links.

Merge / Reorder

  • All-Time Leaders become Club Legends.
  • Premierships move directly below the hero.
  • Detailed Records move to the bottom as the audit layer.

Remove From Headline

  • Ducks move to Quirky Records.
  • Future all-round/fielding performance cards need reliability labels.
  • Do not call anything a club record unless all-time comparison confirms it.
