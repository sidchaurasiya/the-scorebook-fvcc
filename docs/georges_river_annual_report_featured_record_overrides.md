# GRDCC Annual Report Featured Record Overrides

## Purpose

GRDCC uses Historical Excel through `Summer 1971/72`, PlayCricket / PlayHQ from `Summer 1972/73`, and selected Annual Report values for official featured all-time or career records that the source-rule data cannot fully reconstruct.

## Why Overrides Exist

Some official career totals span historical aliases, periods before the supplied source coverage, or records requiring Annual Report reconciliation. Writing those totals into player-season rows would fabricate source data and distort season calculations. They therefore remain a separate, manually reviewable presentation layer.

## Current Approved Overrides

- Harry Milburn: **10,788 career runs**, GRDCC 2024/25 Annual Report page 98.
- Gordon Leslie: **707 career wickets**, GRDCC 2024/25 Annual Report page 100.

## App Behaviour

- Hall of Fame featured `Most Runs` and `Most Wickets` leader displays use approved Annual Report values.
- A subtle `Official Annual Report total` source note identifies overridden values.
- Detailed source-data tables, Player Profile calculations, milestones, season records, 500-run seasons and 50-wicket seasons remain source-rule based.
- Raw Excel and PlayCricket files are unchanged, and the two sources are never summed.

## Caveats

- Every override must identify its Annual Report page, section, reason and approved app scope.
- Future Annual Reports may supersede these values and require a reviewed source-file update.
- Additional records must not be added without evidence and explicit reviewer approval.
