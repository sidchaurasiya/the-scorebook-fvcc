# Hawks club-wide historical career candidate

This review-only expansion preserves the two Briginshaw anchors and adds 61
source-verified existing public identities. It does not create or merge a
player, change historical match policy, or release anything to production.

## Positive evidence gate

The 886 original Career Master player rows, 229 First XI entries, retained
annual/career records, existing approvals and all 1,284 public career rows
were compared. Old FB17B labels are not approvals: some previously treated
already-applied review values as governed. The record-override allowlist and
its review/rejected suppression remain unchanged.

A new source binding requires either a previously approved historical source
mapping, or a unique existing public candidate corroborated by informative
nonzero annual numeric fingerprints in at least two different seasons and a
matching career counter on the same independently identified source record.
Names alone, senior/junior qualifiers, apparent continuity and old audit
recommendations do not authorize a mapping. Glenn Haye and Grant Haye remain
separate. Unresolved First XI names and the known club identity questions
remain audit-only; no ambiguous or protected profile is published.

Each selected metric bundle has its own evidenced cutoff. Supplied annual
books contain both updated career fields and stale carry-forward summaries.
Filenames alone are not cutoffs. T20/One Day/First XI subsets are not added to
club totals. A newer corroborated career counter conflicting with the anchor
plus later digital activity is quarantined rather than resolved by max().

## Metric scopes and persistence

`gwhcc_dated_career_anchors.csv` retains the original two decisions unchanged.
`gwhcc_verified_career_anchors.csv` contains the additional reviewed local
decisions, source row/hash, direct dated evidence and identity references.
`metric_scope` explicitly selects batting, bowling or both. Blank fields in
an unselected bundle are unavailable, not zero.

Batting uses source runs, innings and not-outs plus strictly later verified
contributions; its average uses that same scope. Bowling uses source wickets
and conceded runs plus strictly later contributions; its average uses the
same scope. Optional batting/bowling cutoff columns support independently
dated bundles. Unsupported matches, fielding, HS, fifty/century and detailed
rate fields are unchanged. HOF, Profile and Milestones use the same central
source layer, including after the HOF Not Outs display merge.

Examples: Jarrod Greaves is anchored through 2022/23, retaining the verified
later 52 runs for 7,150. Nathan Bungey retains 2026/27's 50 runs for 3,380.
Matthew Briginshaw retains two later wickets for 535. Anthony Medina receives
batting only: the T. Medina 300-wicket conflict is NOT republished as Anthony.
His governed 62 wickets stay unchanged. Unknown detailed seasons stay absent.

Glen Mahoney's 11,040 runs now have a separate positive, dated, canonical-ID
source decision (Career Master row 498 plus the corroborated career record).
The old review-only record row remains suppressed. This is not a reversal
of the approved-only governance fix. The independent-source decision and
its before/after inventory require customer release approval.

All 21 original FB17C identities and their 101 approved metric decisions,
closed Greg/James/Ahilan/Kash/Sunny decisions and all current-season source
data are preserved. Future weekly updates retain the source decisions and
add only eligible later contributions. Missing counts and duplicate later
keys fail explicitly; no unknown field or average denominator becomes zero.

## Review batches and limits

1. Inherited Briginshaw foundation: two existing reviewed local corrections.
2. Previously approved historical mappings: nine additional identities.
3. Independent same-record/annual corroboration: 52 additional identities.

The outside-repository checkpoint contains separate source manifests,
comprehensive metric and changed-player CSVs, identity/cell proofs, rankings,
tests and browser samples. Review these batches separately; release an
approved subset atomically with its matching tests/fixture, never an
inconsistent code/data intermediate. Rollback is a reviewed normal revert
or exact batch-decision/fixture reversal, not a reset, force push or rebuild.

No current/source datasets, prepared manifests, aliases, partnerships,
fastest innings, hat-tricks, styling or other-club data were regenerated or
changed. Detailed/digital dashboard coverage is not a claim of complete club
history. Unmapped identities, conflicting sources and unsupported metrics
remain visible in the audit classifications rather than silently 'fixed'.

The known legacy Not Outs assertion expects 1,127 comparable prepared rows;
unchanged released data has 1,126. It is reproduced and reported separately,
not concealed by changing data or an unrelated assertion.
