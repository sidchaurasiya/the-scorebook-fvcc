## Navigation and population

Percy retains `grdcc_excel_exact_percy_eldridge`. Phil uses the navigation-only
report key `report_grdcc_annual_2024_25_phil_eldridge`, stored in `report_record_id`
and `player_key`, **not** in `canonical_player_id`. His profile dispatches to a
source-only report view before any primary player/season lookup. Search labels
identify him as an Annual Report record. Neither profile borrows the other's data.

The primary canonical population remains 2,125. Governed display records rise
from 2,127 to 2,128: the same primary records, two existing unlinked report-only
entries, and one newly navigable report-only entry. No source player, appearance,
match, season, worksheet or raw prepared snapshot is invented or rewritten.

## Ranking and thresholds

Percy's report runs are eligible for batting rankings and the 2,000-run band.
His worksheet wickets are a recorded minimum, excluded from complete-career
bowling rankings but eligible for the explicitly labelled 100-wicket minimum band.
Phil's report wickets are eligible for named historical bowling rankings and the
200-wicket band, explicitly labelled Annual Report. Missing metrics do not confer
other eligibility. No exact achievement date or current activity is inferred for
either historical record. Their source uncertainty prevents dated milestone
reconstruction and speculative career badges.

Whole-club/team numerical accounting remains based on original source aggregates,
not the sum of governed display records. The report-only entry never enters
primary/category data or team aggregation. Filtered source scopes cannot reuse an
ALL GRADES total: the complete-career values are unavailable there, with an
explicit pointer to the independently filtered source-season view.

and to prepared/fallback paths. Both Phil and Percy labels remain blocked from

and all unrelated accepted aliases/statistics remain unchanged.
