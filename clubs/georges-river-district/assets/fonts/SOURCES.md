# GRDCC typography assets

Unmodified fonts retrieved 5 November 2026 from the Google Fonts repository.

- Barlow Condensed SemiBold: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/barlowcondensed
- Manrope variable: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope

Both are distributed under the included SIL Open Font License notices. Manrope's
upstream FONTLOG is included. Fonts are embedded by the GRDCC-only stylesheet,
without a runtime third-party font request or a Streamlit static-serving change.
